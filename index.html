Sharks are much more than teeth.

They range from tiny deep-sea hunters to the world's largest fish. They can filter-feed, crush shells, pursue fast prey, navigate huge distances and detect electrical fields produced by living animals.

This atlas turns that science into an explorable experience.

ONE IMPORTANT FACT

Most sharks are not dangerous to humans.

Shark species occupy very different ecological niches, and encounters with people are uncommon relative to the number of people and sharks in the ocean.
